# Code of conduct

Keep project discussions respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. People of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

- Critique technical ideas and evidence, not a person's worth or identity.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, or publish private information without permission.
- Respect requests to stop unwanted contact and keep disagreements proportionate.
- Do not post credentials, private host details, or sensitive reports in public threads.

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation to protect the community. Explain moderation decisions where doing so does not expose private information or increase harm.

Use GitHub's built-in reporting tools for platform abuse or private safety concerns. For an ordinary public moderation request, contact the maintainers in the relevant thread without reposting private material. Vulnerabilities follow the separate private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
